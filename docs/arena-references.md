# BattleBox reference sources

The arena baseline is the 2022 filming of World Championship VII, broadcast in 2023. The project owner authorized the branding and images. This reference set avoids mixing the classic Comedy Central arena, simplified toy arenas, and later live-show revisions.

## Dimensions and layout

The [2022 BattleBots Tournament Rules](https://battlebots.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/BattleBots-Tournament-Rules.2022.1.pdf), sections 6.1.1 and 6.1.3, describe a 48-foot-square enclosure raised two feet above the ground. The floor can have seams, damage, and debris. That enclosure measurement includes boundaries and does not establish a 48-foot unobstructed driving square.

The Upper Deck occupies the far side opposite the entry doors. Its center-facing edge has two reversible screws. The 2022 rules describe a platform several inches high, with short step-up edges at the sides. They do not give its exact width and depth. The [2021 Tournament Rules](https://battlebots.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/BattleBots-Tournament-Rules-Rev.2021.1.1.pdf), section 6.1.3, give approximate dimensions of 16 by 8 feet and six inches of height. The licensed guide also gives a 16-by-8-foot footprint. Those dimensions are the reconstruction baseline, rather than a measured WCVII survey.

Section 7.5.4 of the 2022 rules describes the Upper Deck screws lifting robots onto the platform, then reversing to push robots off when one is on it. Screws are hazards. The platform floor itself is not classified as a hazard.

## Hazard operation

The licensed *BattleBots Official Guide*, printed page 12, confirms four corner pulverizers, two controlled by each team. It also states that killsaws emerge in the last minute. Page 13 shows reversible serrated steel augers, paired circular saw blades rising through narrow floor slots, and the Upper Deck's two front screw shafts. The guide's preview images remain available even though its old product page redirects to a store error page. [Guide page 12](https://embed.cdn.pais.scholastic.com/v1/products/identifiers/isbn/9781338822021/see-inside/10/renditions/1600), [guide page 13](https://embed.cdn.pais.scholastic.com/v1/products/identifiers/isbn/9781338822021/see-inside/11/renditions/1600).

The user selected three-minute fights, killsaws in the final minute, and screws and pulverizers available earlier. Three-minute bouts also match the [Discovery show description](https://www.discovery.com/shows/battlebots).

Exact saw-bank coordinates, the total number of WCVII saw banks, random activation intervals, auger RPM, hammer force, and damage values are not established by the reviewed primary sources. Game placements and forces are approximations. Automatic hammer targeting would be a game adaptation because the real pulverizers are team-controlled.
